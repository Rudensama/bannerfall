## 2. Core Loop [LOCKED]

- **Turn-based.** Field advances one step after a shot *fully resolves* (including all gravity cascades). Sliding and shelf-swapping are **free**.
- **Lanes.** Cannon snaps to discrete lanes, fires **straight up**.
- **No clock.** Thinking is free; acting always advances the field *and* spends a ball.
- **The race.** Reach the score goal before the wall breaks **or** the ammo pool runs dry.

**Moment-to-moment:** read board → slide to a lane → (optionally swap shelf) → fire → shot resolves, chains/cascades score → banners apply → field steps, spawn counter ticks → repeat.
