## 1. High Concept

A turn-based, lane-defense color-matcher with a Balatro-style roguelite layer. Colored balls descend in rows toward a cannon on a castle wall. The field advances one step every time the player fires. Match colors to destroy connected groups; survive each stage long enough to hit its **score goal** — using a **finite, shuffled ammo pool**. Build a temporary loadout of special balls and passive **Banners** between stages. Runs are self-contained; power is wiped each run. Between runs, the **Dock** sells permanent starting conditions and cosmetics.

**One-line pitch:** Bubble-shooter matching + Tetris next/hold + lane defense + Balatro run-building.

**Platform:** Mobile (portrait). **Genre:** Casual / roguelite puzzle.

### Two lose conditions [LOCKED]
1. **Wall breached** — any ball reaches the wall (spatial pressure).
2. **Out of ammo** — the stage's ammo pool empties before the score goal is reached (resource pressure).

A stage is a race against *both* at once. The whole game is tuning which threat dominates on a given stage.
