## 8. In-Run Shop (between stages) [LOCKED]

**Shop size [LOCKED]:** each between-stage shop offers only **3–5 items** (candidate: **4**, tunable at MVP), drawn from the pool below by **rarity weighting** (§5a/§9: commons appear often, rares gated, legendaries scarce). That's the **per-stage ceiling on purchases** — you buy from what the roll gives you, not the full catalog, so every shop is a scarcity decision. The rare **+Banner slot** upgrade, when it rolls, occupies one of these item slots. *(Selling a Banner back is a separate action and does not consume an offered slot. Shop reroll is deferred to the monetization pass, Step 7.)*

No new colors. Instead the shop deepens the ammo pool you have. The offered items are drawn from:

- **Increase ammo count (uniform)** — raises **every color's** count by the same amount, so colors always stay **equal** (e.g. 4 colors × 5 → 4 colors × 6). Keeps the field's spawn distribution balanced and avoids the degenerate single-/skewed-color case. The "I need a deeper ammo pool" lever.
- **Upgrade a color's score value** — make a chosen color a **high-score ball**. Stacks. The scoring-build lever (set up big chains in your money color). Does *not* touch counts, so it never unbalances the field.
- **Special balls** (§5a) added into the ammo pool (permanent for the run).
- **Banners** (§9).
- **In-run upgrades:** **+Banner slot** (expand from the base 3, §9) — a **rare** shop appearance with **escalating cost** per additional slot, so going wide on Banners is a real, gated investment, not a default. Plus +Chain Shot X, +Bombard R, etc. *(Note: +shelf slot and +conveyor preview are intentionally NOT sold here — they come only from the Long March / Quartermaster's Flag Banners (§9), to avoid redundancy.)*
- **Sell Banners back [LOCKED]** — any held Banner can be sold back to the shop for **half its purchase price** (rounded down), refunded as coins. Frees a slot and enables build pivots when a better Banner appears. Always a net loss vs. what you paid, so there's no buy/sell farming exploit. **Sell-back is Banner-only — specials (§5a) cannot be sold.**

### 8a. Currency [LOCKED]
- **Coins = leftover ammo at stage end** (balls you didn't need to draw to reach the goal). Hitting the goal efficiently → more coins. Since the ammo pool restores next stage, leftover ammo isn't "saved" — it's purely a per-stage **efficiency reward**.
- **Banners can modify coins** (multiply, flat bonus per stage end / per Banner held / per shot fired — see §9).
- *(Coins are the in-run soft currency and reset each run. The persistent meta currency is **Renown**, earned per run and spent at the Dock — see §10 / §10a.)*

> **8b — RESOLVED [LOCKED].** No per-ball copies — count upgrades are **uniform across all colors** (colors stay equal), so no single super-ball can exist. Per-color **value** upgrades stack freely; runaway value scaling is *intended* (that's the roguelite build). Field stays balanced because counts never skew.
