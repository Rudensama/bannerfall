## 10. The Dock — Meta Progression [LOCKED]

Between **runs**, not stages. Permanent, deliberately **off** the in-run power curve:

- **Starting conditions:** a **fixed ammo pool** every fresh run begins with. Different pools carry **different numbers of color types** and base counts (start small — e.g. few colors, ~5 each; expand the *baseline* over time). More color types = harder but higher scoring ceiling.
- **Cosmetics:** cannon / wall / conveyor / banner skins.
- **No in-run power for sale here.** Banners and specials are *earned through play* (entering the in-run shop pool).
- **Currency: Renown [LOCKED].** The Dock is priced in **Renown** — a persistent meta currency earned per run, scaled by performance (stages cleared, score, bosses beaten). Renown is the *only* meta currency; it buys starting conditions + cosmetics, never in-run power. (In-run **Coins** (§8a) are separate and reset each run.)

### 10a. Monetization (Step 7) [LOCKED]

**Model: hybrid-casual, fairness-locked.** Rewarded ads do the heavy lifting; light IAP fills the margins. **Iron rule [LOCKED]: nothing bought with money touches in-run power.** Money buys *time, convenience, and cosmetics* — never a stronger run. A roguelite audience is the most pay-to-win-allergic crowd in mobile; the genre sells itself on fair runs, so breaking this loses the retention core. This shape is also what hyper-casual publishers (Voodoo / Supersonic / etc.) look for and is cleanly measurable (rewarded-ad engagement, ARPDAU).

#### Currencies [LOCKED] — two only, no premium/gem currency
- **Coins** — in-run soft currency (= leftover ammo, §8a). Resets each run; spent in the between-stage shop (§8).
- **Renown** — persistent meta currency, earned per run by performance; spent at the Dock (§10) on starting conditions + cosmetics.
- **Real money buys bundles / cosmetics / remove-ads directly** — no intermediate gem currency to convert. Cleaner to build, reads as more honest, and a first commercial title doesn't need gems. (Can layer in later if the numbers demand it.)

#### Rewarded ads (opt-in — the workhorse)
| Placement | Offer | Fairness |
|---|---|---|
| **Coin doubler** | Stage end: watch to double that stage's leftover-ammo coins. | Pure economy boost, optional, no power injection. |
| **Shop reroll** | First reroll of the 3–5 shop costs **coins** (escalating); further rerolls via **rewarded ad**. | Still pays the coin economy; the ad buys another roll of the same RNG, not a chosen item. |
| **Extra shop item** | Watch to reveal one **bonus** offered item (a 6th slot) this shop. | RNG'd, not chosen — a pick, not power-on-demand. |
| **Continue after loss** | **Casual mode only**, **once per run**: watch to survive a wall breach (clears the front row, refills nothing). | Contained to one revive; **disabled in Daily/competitive** (see guardrail). |

#### Forced ads (sparing)
- **Interstitials at run breaks only** — on the run-end → Dock transition, capped (e.g. ≥2-min interval or every 2nd run). **Never mid-run** (a thinky portrait puzzle can't take a mid-board interstitial).
- **No gameplay banner ads** — they'd clutter the board. Banners, if any, live on menu/Dock screens only.
- Deliberately lighter than a true hyper-casual title — this genre rewards a slightly more premium feel.

#### IAP
- **Remove Ads** (one-time) — kills all forced ads; rewarded ads stay opt-in. The premium-conversion anchor.
- **Content bundles** — early-unlock **Banner Bundle**, **Pool Bundle**, etc.: content otherwise earned by playing. Sells *time*, not power.
- **Cosmetics** — cannon / wall / conveyor / Banner skins (§10); high-margin, fully fair; a few can be IAP-exclusive.
- *(Optional later)* one-time **Starter Pack** offer.

#### Guardrail [LOCKED]
**No monetization affects seeded competitive play.** In Daily Challenge and any leaderboard mode, **ad-continue and ad-reroll are disabled** — ads aren't equally available to everyone, and rerolls would diverge the shared seed (§11). Monetization lives in **Normal and Endless only**. This is what keeps leaderboards trustworthy and the fairness promise intact.

> **§10a — RESOLVED [LOCKED].** Hybrid-casual, fairness-locked. Two currencies (Coins in-run, Renown meta), no gems. Rewarded ads: coin-doubler, shop reroll (coins→ad), extra item, casual-only single continue. Forced ads: capped run-break interstitials only. IAP: remove-ads, time-saving content bundles, cosmetics. Competitive modes are monetization-free. Rates/caps/prices are tuning targets for soft-launch.
